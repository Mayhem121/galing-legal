Support

Kumusta! Here is help with the usual questions. Anything else: info@codesynap.com (we answer within two working days).

I got a new phone. Where is my progress?

Galing keeps your progress on the phone. If the new phone was restored from your iCloud or Google backup, everything is already there. Otherwise, on the old phone open Profile → Backup & restore → Export backup file, save it to iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Files, then on the new phone tap Restore from a backup on the welcome screen.

I paid for Premium but the units are locked

Open Profile → Restore purchases while signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought with. Premium follows that account, not the backup file.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not inside the app. iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions, or play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds are requested from Apple or Google.

How do I delete my data?

Profile → Erase all my data removes everything Galing stored on the phone. Deleting the app does the same. We hold no copy.

A word or translation looks wrong

Please tell us which language, unit and word. Native speakers review every report and corrections ship in the next content update, which does not need an app update.

Speaking exercises say the microphone is unavailable

Check that Galing is allowed to use the microphone and speech recognition in your phone's settings, and that a speech service for Filipino is installed (on Android this is the Google app). You can also switch speaking exercises off under Profile → Preferences.
