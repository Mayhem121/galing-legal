Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026 · Applies to the Galing app for iOS, Android and the web preview

The short version: Galing has no accounts. Your name, lessons, streaks and settings are stored on your own phone. We do not run a server that collects information about you, and we do not use analytics or advertising trackers. The only companies that receive anything are Apple or Google when you buy a subscription, and the payment service that manages it (RevenueCat), and that is limited to what a purchase needs.

1. Who we are

Galing is published by Code Synap LLC. Questions about this policy: info@codesynap.com.

2. What the app stores, and where

DataWhere it livesWhy
The first name you type (optional), the language you learn, your goals, daily target and reminder timeOn your device onlyTo personalise the app
Lesson results, XP, coins, streaks, words learned, outfits, quest progressOn your device onlyTo run the app and show your progress
Offline lesson audio you downloadOn your device onlyOffline use
Backup files you exportWherever you save them (Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive…)Moving to a new phone

Because this data lives on your phone, it is included in your phone's own backups (iCloud Backup on iOS, Google backup on Android) under Apple's or Google's terms. We never receive a copy.

3. Subscriptions and purchases

Premium is sold through Apple's App Store or Google Play using their in-app purchase systems. Apple or Google process the payment and know what you bought; we never see your card details. To check whether a purchase is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives a random identifier generated on your device (not your name or email), your purchase receipt, your device type and app version. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy. This is the only information about you that leaves your phone to a service we use. It is used to unlock what you paid for and to restore purchases on a new device, and RevenueCat also shows us overall sales and subscriber totals, which are never linked to who you are.

4. Microphone and speech recognition

Speaking exercises are optional. If you allow it, the app uses your phone's built-in speech recognition (provided by Apple or Google) to check what you said. Audio is handled by that service under Apple's or Google's policies and may be processed on their servers to recognise speech. Galing never records, stores or receives your voice.

5. Notifications

Daily reminders and the word of the day are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to us, and you can switch them off at any time in the app or in your phone's settings.

6. "Ask Pao"

The tutor in this version answers from the course content stored in the app. Your questions are not sent anywhere. If we add an online tutor in the future, we will update this policy first and ask before any question leaves your device.

7. What we do not do

  • No accounts, passwords or email addresses.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and no cross-app tracking.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal data.
  • No access to contacts, location, photos or the camera.

8. Children

Galing is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since nothing is stored with us, there is nothing for a parent to request; deleting the app removes its data.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything: Profile → "Erase all my data", or uninstall the app.
  • Take your data with you: Profile → "Backup & restore" → "Export backup file".
  • Purchases: managed through your Apple or Google account; RevenueCat data can be deleted on request by writing to us.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK GDPR, California's CCPA, or the Philippine Data Privacy Act) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we hold none, these requests are usually fulfilled by the steps above. You can still write to us and we will help.

10. Security

Your data is protected by your phone's own security (passcode, encryption, backups). Keep exported backup files somewhere private; they contain your progress.

11. Changes

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its effective date.
